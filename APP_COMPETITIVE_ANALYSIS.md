```
Women Safety App (9 Steps):
```

### 3. **Multi-Sensory Trigger System**

**We Support 5 Trigger Methods:**

1. **SOS Button Press** - Visible in app
   - Holdable vs. tapable modes
   - Requires confirmation (prevent accidental triggers)

2. **Device Shake Detection** - Always listening
   - 3+ shakes = automatic SOS
   - Calibration for different users
   - Disabled in safe situations

3. **Voice Activation** - Always listening
   - Custom voice command ("Help" or "Emergency")
   - Offline voice detection (Dart speech_to_text)
   - Works even with phone in pocket

4. **Home Screen Panic Widget** - 1-tap from lock screen
   - Unique to Flutter + HomeWidget framework
   - No need to unlock phone
   - Most direct access possible

5. **Volume Button Long Press** - Hardware accessibility
   - No app interaction needed
   - Works even with app in background
   - 3+ seconds = discrete trigger

**Competitors Typically Offer:**
- SOS button only (some add shake)
- No voice activation
- Limited widget support
- No volume button integration
