# Code of Conduct

We follow the Contributor Covenant.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior:
- Inclusive and respectful language
- Graceful acceptance of constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for users needing safety tools

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or insults
- Publishing others' private information
- Sexualized or violent content

## Enforcement
Report violations to the maintainers via a confidential channel (email placeholder: `maintainers@example.com`). Maintainers will review and act in good faith.

## Scope
Applies in all project spaces, GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, and external platforms where the project is represented.
